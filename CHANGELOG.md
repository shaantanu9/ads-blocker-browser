# Changelog

## 0.1.0-alpha — 2026-11-13

First Android MVP release.

- Local WebView browsing with a curated ad/tracker blocking starter set.
- Cosmetic ad hiding, popup prevention, Safe Browsing, and per-site Allow ads controls.
- Local tabs, private tabs, bookmarks, history, and clear-data controls.
- No account, analytics, advertising, VPN, proxy, or project-operated browsing server.

Known limits: the filter set is intentionally small; signed rule updates, full hardware benchmarking, and physical-device
QA are pending. This build is not a claim of complete ad-blocking or production/store readiness.
